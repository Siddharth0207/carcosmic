Got Questions About The Course? We've Got Answers.

Everything you need to know about our process, pricing,
and how we work together
Do I need prior automotive or detailing experience to join?

No prior experience is necessary! Our training is engineered for passionate car enthusiasts, beginners, and existing car wash or workshop owners looking to scale. We start from foundational paint chemistry, clear-coat dynamics, and machine handling up to advanced multi-stage correction and PPF edge wrapping.

Will I get hands-on practical training on real vehicles?

Yes! Over 80% of your course time is spent on the workshop floor working directly on live vehicles. You won't just practice on scrap metal panels; you will operate rotary and dual-action polishers, apply 9H ceramic coatings, and wrap TPU film on actual car curves under expert supervision.

What certification will I receive upon completing the course?

You will receive an official Car Cosmic Professional Detailer & PPF Specialist Certificate. Our academy operates under ISO 9001:2015 quality standards and IDA-aligned best practices, providing you with instant trust and prestige when opening your studio or quoting clients.

How does Car Cosmic help me start my own detailing studio?

Business incubation is a cornerstone of our 6-day program. CEO Raj Deshmukh guides you through studio location analysis, floor plan layout, hex lighting installation, optimal capital budgeting, service pricing margins, and client acquisition scripts. Plus, you get lifetime technical support and wholesale pricing on machines and chemicals.

Is accommodation provided for outstation students travelling to Surat?

Surat is easily accessible by flight, train, and expressways. While travel allowances and hotel stays are not covered by the academy and must be borne by the student, we provide complete local assistance by recommending trusted, comfortable hotels and guest houses within 5 to 10 minutes of our academy for a smooth, hassle-free training experience.
